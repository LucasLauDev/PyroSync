## PyroSync

We developed PyroSync, an interactive web platform that harmonizes MODIS and VIIRS active fire hotspots from the early 2000s to the present into a unified Burning Activity interactive Map and Calendar. It directly addresses the challenge by cross-calibrating differing satellite resolutions and sensitivities, providing a consistent timeline where genuine fire patterns instead of sensor differences take center stage. Users can define any area of interest by drawing boundaries or entering coordinates on an interactive map to explore regional fire activity with accessible color ramps. Its importance lies in preventing false alarms caused by satellite transitions while analysing current fires against historical medians, empowering emergency responders and land managers to detect critical anomalies early and protect communities before wildfires become catastrophic.
